Frequently asked questions

What is Moti?

Moti is a cozy virtual pet that lives in your backpack. Feed, water and play with your pet, keep notebooks and a sketchbook, play mini games, and (with Moti Pro) chat with an AI buddy that summarizes lectures, writes notes and makes quizzes.

What is free and what is in Moti Pro?

  • Free: cat and dog, 2 notebooks with 5 pages each, 5 sketchbook drawings, mini games, widgets.
  • Moti Pro: AI chat, lecture summaries, notes and quizzes, all pets and changing your pet, 5 notebooks with 15 pages each, up to 200 drawings.

How do I subscribe or restore my purchase?

Tap Pro in the top bar to open the subscription screen. If you already subscribed on this Apple ID (for example after reinstalling the app or switching phones), tap Restore purchases on that screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Moti on your iPhone and choose Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which files can I send to the AI buddy?

PDF, Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx) and plain text files. Open the Study section or the chat, attach a lecture and ask for a summary, notes for your notebook or a quiz.

The AI does not answer or shows an error.

Check your internet connection and that Moti Pro is active (use Restore purchases if needed). Very large files may be shortened. If the problem continues, email us with a short description.

Is my data private?

Your pet, notebooks, drawings and diary are stored on your device. Only chat messages and files you choose to send to the AI are sent (through our server) to the AI provider to generate a reply. Your personal diary is never sent. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Open Profile → Delete account in the app. This erases the data stored on your device. You can also email us with a request.
