Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Moti (the “App”) handles when you use it, why we handle it, and what choices you have.

1. Who we are

The App is provided by Moti Team (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at support.moti.team@gmail.com .

2. Information we handle

• Account details: your nickname, email address, chosen pet, and avatar (an emoji, a color, or a photo you pick). • Content you create: notebook pages, stickers, drawings, planner entries, savings goals, and diary entries. • Messages and files you send to the AI helper: chat messages and any lectures or documents you attach. • Game data: your pet's name and stats, inventory, coins, and mini-game scores. • Purchases: subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details, only confirmation that a subscription is active. • Technical data: we do not use advertising identifiers, and at the time of writing the App does not include third-party analytics or advertising tools. A random installation identifier is sent with AI requests only to limit usage and prevent abuse.

3. How we use information

We use information to run the App and its features (pet care, notebook, sketchbook, planner, mini-games, study tools), to generate AI replies, summaries, notes, and quizzes, to keep the App secure and fix problems, and to comply with the law. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. AI features and third parties

When you chat with your pet or attach a file, the text of your message or file, together with limited context (your pet's name and state, sticker texts, and a short excerpt of your notebook), is sent over an encrypted connection to an AI service provider, Anthropic, which generates the reply. This happens through our server. The provider processes this data to deliver the response under its own terms and privacy policy. Please do not send content you do not want processed this way. If you do not use the AI features, no such data is sent. Your personal diary is never sent to the AI helper.

5. Where data is stored

In the current version, your account, pet progress, and content are stored on your device. If you delete the App or use “Delete account” in your Profile, this local data is erased. If we add cloud sync or server-side accounts, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

6. Retention

We keep information only as long as it is needed for the purposes above. Files you attach to the AI helper are used to produce the response and are not kept by the App after you close it. Our AI provider may retain data for a limited period according to its own policy.

7. Children

The App is intended for people aged 13 and older (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, if higher). If you are younger, please use the App only with the permission and supervision of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can delete your data in the App (Profile → Delete account) or contact us at support.moti.team@gmail.com.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the data stored on your device is protected by iOS file protection. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International transfers

AI processing may take place in countries other than yours, including the United States.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will publish the new version in the App and change the date at the top.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: support.moti.team@gmail.com .
